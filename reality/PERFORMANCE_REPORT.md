## Interpretation

- These numbers come from pure, dependency-free Python, which is the reference implementation.
  They establish a correctness baseline and a scaling baseline; they are not a performance claim.
- Arithmetic for a 60 FPS scene: 10^4 updates per frame needs 6 x 10^5 updates/s and 10^6 needs
  6 x 10^7 updates/s. The measured kernels above run at roughly 10^5 to 10^7 simple updates/s on this
  machine, so pure Python covers small scenes only. Larger scenes need C++ (in-Blender) or GPU
  kernels; the porting order is listed in `ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- Adaptive or multi-resolution methods and GPU backends are not implemented; no speed-up is claimed.
- Accuracy/performance trade-offs are expressed through explicit step sizes and tolerances
  (see `NUMERICAL_METHODS.md`). Shrinking `dt` buys accuracy at a known cost, never silently.
